# Product Overview

## FSM - Field Service Management System

This is a comprehensive field service management application designed for pest control businesses. The system manages the complete workflow from lead generation to service completion and payment collection.

## Core Features

- **Lead Management**: Track potential customers from initial contact through conversion
- **Service Scheduling**: Assign and manage pest control services with technicians
- **Customer Management**: Maintain customer profiles and service history
- **Employee Management**: Track technician assignments and performance
- **Inventory Management**: Monitor pest control chemicals and supplies
- **Payment Processing**: Handle various payment modes (UPI, Cash, Pre-paid)
- **Project Tracking**: Manage ongoing pest control projects
- **Reporting & Analytics**: Dashboard with KPIs and performance metrics

## Business Domain

The application is specifically tailored for pest control service companies with features like:
- Service types: Termite Control, Cockroach Control, Bed Bug Treatment, Rodent Control, General Pest Control
- Multi-branch operations (Kochi, Calicut, Thrissur, Trivandrum, Palakkad, Munnar)
- Technician field operations with cash settlement workflows
- Chemical inventory management with reorder levels
- Quote generation and tracking system

## User Types

- **Administrators**: Full system access for business management
- **Sales Executives**: Lead management and quote generation
- **Technicians**: Field service execution and payment collection
- **Managers**: Branch operations and reporting oversight
